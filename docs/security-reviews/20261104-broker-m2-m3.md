## Findings
| # | Severity | Area | Finding | Disposition | Fix / reason | Regression test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | Response scanning | With allow-once on an imported credential, the broker injected through a one-shot binding that was never stored. The minted-token scan looked only at stored bindings, so tokens the upstream minted reached the tab. | fixed `4a99a2d` | Scan on `seclab.injected` or a bound host. A non-text credentialed body that looks like it holds a token is blocked. | `test_allow_once_binding_still_scans_minted_tokens` |
| 2 | Medium | Approvals | Binding approvals were coalesced by origin. A concurrent request with a different path, method or header joined the prompt and received the real value. | fixed `4a99a2d` | Approval key includes method, path, inject header and format. A decision never releases a request the approved binding does not cover. | `test_binding_approvals_are_not_coalesced_across_requests` |
| 3 | Medium | Matcher | Inside a tunnel, an HTTP/1 absolute-form authority was not checked and was forwarded as-is, so the origin routed on another vhost (domain fronting, or a credential reaching another vhost). | fixed `4a99a2d` | `r.authority` is checked for every HTTP version; HTTP/1 is always forwarded in origin-form. | `test_absolute_form_inside_tunnel_rejected` |
| 4 | Low | Robustness | mitmproxy logs a hook exception and lets the flow continue (fail open). An unencodable minted value poisoned the credential store and disabled reflection scanning for credentials provided later. | fixed `4a99a2d` | `@fail_closed` on every hook; UTF-8 validation of values; `_rebuild` builds new matchers before swapping them in. | `test_derive_rejects_bad_values_and_store_survives` |
| 5 | Low | DoS | Derived credentials were unbounded, and each one recompiled the matcher. | fixed `4a99a2d` | Limits: 64 derived credentials per parent, 1024 in total; past them the token is redacted. | `test_derived_credentials_are_capped` |
| 6 | Low | Response scanning | Response trailers were not scanned. | fixed `4a99a2d` | Trailers are scanned (block on any hit). Request trailers count as a misplaced sentinel location. | none: hypercorn fixture cannot send trailers (accepted gap) |
| 7 | Medium | Derived credentials (1b) | On credentialed flows, `Set-Cookie` session cookies passed through. They are a derived credential that can be replayed on any path. | fixed `b398fed` | Strip `Set-Cookie`, `Set-Cookie2`, `Authentication-Info` and `Proxy-Authentication-Info` on credentialed flows, and audit `session.stripped`. | `test_session_cookies_from_credentialed_requests_are_stripped` |
| 8 | Low | Robustness (1b) | `_hook_failed` wrote the audit event before containing the flow, so if auditing also failed, the flow went through unchanged. `request()` passed a flow that had no classification. | fixed `b398fed` | Contain the flow first, then audit inside its own `try`; deny unclassified flows. | covered by fail-closed path; no separate fault-injection test |
| 9 | Low | Proxy auth | Rotating the credential does not tear down established WebSockets or flows already held. | accepted | Rotation is rare (tab restart). Recorded in the README's open issues. | n/a |

The reviewer found **no issues** in these areas:
- proxy auth ordering: auth happens before any hold, prompt, DNS lookup or connection;
- request matching and normalisation;
- placement-only injection;
- `server_connect` coverage and IP pinning (DNS rebinding);
- control socket permissions and error scrubbing;
- Dockerfile pinning.
